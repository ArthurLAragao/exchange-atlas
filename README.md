# Exchange Atlas

An independent exchange planner for UNIFOR Computer Science students comparing 31 universities across eight countries.

- Explore campus life, city guides, documented English-taught computing courses, and living-cost estimates.
- Compare universities and browse salary and freelance benchmarks by market.
- Model a semester budget or freelance revenue using your own inputs.

[Open Exchange Atlas](https://arthurlaragao.github.io/exchange-atlas/)

## Open locally

Open `index.html` in a browser. The directory, filters, photos and calculators are bundled with the site. External source links and campus tours require internet access.

## Sources and photo credits

The destination and career guides link to their research sources. The hero image is an AI-generated campus illustration. The Wikimedia Commons photographs below were resized and converted to WebP for the site.
